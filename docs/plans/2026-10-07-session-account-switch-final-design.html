Session account switch · final design

Reference for the epic. A Claude session hits its usage limit on work2claude. A limit strip above the composer replaces the in-chat error. You can switch the session to another Claude account, after a confirm that spells out what carries over, or resume on the same account at reset, by hand or automatically. The strip disappears once the session moves on, leaving a one-line divider in the transcript.

Rules

  • The strip replaces the in-chat error. The synthetic "you've hit your limit" reply is not rendered as a message. The unanswered user message gets a small held tag and is re-sent by whichever recovery runs.
  • The strip is the only limit UI in the chat. It has no dismiss button and leaves when the session is unblocked: switched, resumed, or the reset time passes.
  • A manual switch always confirms with the handoff dialog (frame C), because it costs a cold prompt cache and drops reasoning. The divider's What carried over? reopens the same summary read-only, including after an auto-switch.
  • Auto-switch guardrails: at most one switch per limited turn; skip accounts known to be limited until their reset; always leave a divider; never switch in the middle of a turn.
  • Resume at reset is per session and survives daemon restarts. It is re-checked when it fires (account still limited means stay waiting, session already moved means cancel).
  • Phone: the strip keeps both full labels on full-width buttons; the dropdown and dialog become bottom sheets.
