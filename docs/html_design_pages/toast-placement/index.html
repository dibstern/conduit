Toast placement

Toasts vs Q&A and permission alerts, phone and desktop

Goal. Toasts get one home of their own that never collides with the in-session ask cards (bottom of the transcript), the keyboard, the session sheet, or the cross-session "needs attention" alert. Every frame below shows the worst case at once: keyboard open, a permission card waiting, an error toast, and two other sessions needing you.

B. Attention in the chrome, toasts on top (recommended)

2

Split by kind. "Two sessions need you" is state, so it becomes a chip in the top bar that stays until handled. A new arrival is an event, so it also fires one toast with an Open action. Toasts are the only floating thing, hanging under the top bar. Asks keep the bottom.
