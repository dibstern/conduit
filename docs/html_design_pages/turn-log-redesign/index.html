Turn log: wider exploration

Seven directions × collapsed, live, expanded × desktop and phone

You're right: skill chapters can't be known. Nothing links a step to a skill. Today's code guesses: skillChapters (utils/turns.ts:742) says a skill "governs" from its load until the next skill loads or the turn ends. But a loaded skill stays in context for the rest of the session. Two can apply at once, and the model can load one and then ignore it. So the "1m 12s in · 3m 0s" timing in today's skills list, and the strip highlight it drives, are guesses too.

What we can honestly show: a skill load is an event, with a name and a time. Every design below treats it that way and drops the span. The ✦ N skills toggle goes everywhere.

Each direction below shows Collapsed, Live and Expanded, on desktop and phone. Sections 2, 3 and 6 answer your container question: a single panel, a recessed tray, and a raised sheet. Sections 4 and 5 change how the log is structured. Sections 1 and 7 change only where the summary lives.
