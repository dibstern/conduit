The bug: when you send a message and autocompaction kicks in, the transcript shows a Working turn card plus a separate "Compacting…" notice. Nothing is working on your request yet, so the card lies, and the two statuses compete.

The catch: at the moment you press send, conduit can't know whether compaction will happen. So the sent state has to be neutral enough to turn into either "compacting" or "working" without a flash of the wrong one. That's why this page has two sections.

Every card is a 3-frame storyboard: 1 Sent → 2 Compacting → 3 Replying. Use the toggle top right for dark mode.

S1 Neutral presence + C1 Live seam.

  • No wrong first guess. The pulse commits to nothing, so it can become either state without a flash.
