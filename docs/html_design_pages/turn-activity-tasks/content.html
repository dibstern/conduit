  1. Which turn owns a task. The turn that launched it (today's liveLaunchedTasks). A task started by a subagent belongs to that subagent, and through it to the same turn.
  2. Tasks outlive turns. A watcher can run for hours across later turns. Design 1 draws this directly; the others need a "still running" state on a settled turn, and the newest turn should point back to it.
  3. The session bar. Keep the pills there too, or shrink them to a count that jumps to the owning turn (design 3). Showing the full detail in both places is noise.
  4. Phone. Designs 3, 7 and 8 fit a 390px card as drawn. Designs 1, 5 and 9 need a different phone layout.
